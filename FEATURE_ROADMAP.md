# ResumeIQ - Comprehensive Feature Roadmap

## 🎯 TIER 1: HIGH IMPACT (Quick Wins)

### Resume Features
- **Resume Templates** - Pre-built ATS-friendly templates (Modern, Minimalist, Creative)
- **Download Resume** - Export as PDF, DOCX, JSON
- **Resume Versioning** - Track and compare different resume versions
- **Duplicate Resume** - Clone existing resume for experimentation
- **Resume Preview** - Real-time preview as you edit
- **Drag & Drop Editor** - Reorder sections, move achievements around

### Enhanced Analysis
- **Section-by-Section Analysis** - Analyze summary, experience, skills separately
- **Keyword Density** - Show most important keywords for the role
- **ATS Optimization Score** - Specific ATS compatibility (0-100)
- **Grammar & Spelling Check** - Real-time spell check
- **Readability Score** - Flesch-Kincaid reading level
- **Action Verb Suggestions** - Replace weak verbs with strong ones

### Job Matching
- **Job Scraping** - Integrate with LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor
- **Save Jobs** - Bookmark interesting opportunities
- **Match History** - View past job matches and scores
- **Batch Job Matching** - Analyze multiple jobs at once
- **Salary Insights** - Show average salary for matched positions
